# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

## Our pledge

Everyone is welcome to take part, whatever their experience, background or identity. We want issues, pull requests and discussions to be friendly, patient and useful, including for people asking their first question.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the code, not the person, and explain why.
- Accept that a design was decided differently from how you would have done it.
- Help newcomers find their way.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Insults, harassment, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after the maintainer has decided.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainer, Naufal Raism, at **naufalraismajid@gmail.com**. Reports are read only by the maintainer and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Depending on what happened, the maintainer may ask for a change, hide or delete comments, close a thread, or block someone from the project, temporarily or for good.
